How it works

How planning with Sorry on PTO works

You bring the dream trip and the dates. I bring the research, bookings and backup. Here’s what working together looks like.

01

Book a free consult

Your first consult is free. Use my booking page to tell me where you’d like to go, who’s traveling, your timing and budget. We’ll talk about how you like to travel—your must-dos, your hard nos and the trips you still talk about.

02

I design your trip

I research the route, hotels and experiences that match your style, then share a custom proposal. We refine it together until it feels right. No cookie-cutter itineraries.

03

We book it

I book your hotels and the pieces we agree on, and request any partner perks your hotel offers through Fora. You get one clear itinerary with everything in one place.

04

You log off

Travel with support. If plans change or something needs fixing, you have an advocate who will contact the hotel or help find a solution.

What I plan

  • Custom, multi-city itineraries, including Japan—my specialty
  • Honeymoons, anniversaries and couples trips
  • Luxury hotel stays with Fora partner perks (often breakfast, credits and upgrades when available)
  • Group bookings, wedding blocks, corporate events and retreats
  • Hotel-only bookings when you just want the perks and the backup

Pricing

Your first consult is free. Every trip is different, so planning pricing depends on what you need. Please reach out for details. We’ll talk it through on that free consult, before you commit to anything.
