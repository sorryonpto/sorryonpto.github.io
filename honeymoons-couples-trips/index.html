Honeymoons & couples trips

Honeymoons and couples trips, planned so you can just be together

You’ve spent months planning a wedding, or years saying “someday.” Let me plan the trip. You show up, I handle the details, and the only thing on your calendar is each other.

Plan our trip

Romance with room to breathe

A beach morning, a long lunch, an adventure you’ll still be laughing about. I balance rest and play so neither of you comes home needing a vacation from your vacation.

Hotels that feel special

Through Fora’s luxury partner programs, many hotels offer perks for my clients: complimentary breakfast, resort credits and upgrades when available. Always say it’s your honeymoon—I make sure the hotel knows.

One person to call

Flight changed? Room not right? You shouldn’t be on hold on your honeymoon. I’ll reach out on your behalf and keep things moving.

Ideas to get you dreaming

Where couples love to go

  • Japan — Tokyo energy, a ryokan or onsen stay, quiet Kyoto mornings. See my Japan planning.
  • Amalfi Coast & Lake Como — Long dinners, boat days and cliffside views.
  • The Caribbean — Beautiful beaches balanced with real adventure; ask me about Barbados.
  • The Alps & the Dolomites — Mountain air, hiking, cozy hotels and great food.
  • Your own idea — Tell me what you’re both into and I’ll take it from there.

Also planning

Anniversaries, babymoons and wedding blocks

Celebrating a milestone anniversary, a babymoon, or a destination wedding? I also handle group bookings and wedding room blocks. You focus on what matters. I’ll handle the rest, so your guests simply arrive.
