How booking works

Three easy steps to your next OOO

  1. 01

    Open the portal

    Tap Start booking. Returning clients sign in; new travelers use the link in the form to join.

  2. 02

    Share your trip

    Tell me your dates, who’s coming, your investment range and your wishlist. The more I know, the better it fits.

  3. 03

    I handle the rest

    We talk it through on your free consult, then I plan, book and stay on call while you travel.

What to have ready

A few things that help

  • Dates
    Exact or flexible, plus how many nights
  • Travelers
    Who’s coming, and any kids’ ages
  • Investment range
    A comfortable range for the whole trip
  • Wishlist
    Places, hotels, food and must-dos

Most of the journeys I design are fully custom luxury and group trips. Booking with a Fora advisor means perks at many luxury hotels, like breakfast and upgrades when available, and I handle the details so you don’t have to.
